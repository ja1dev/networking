Terminal labs

Real CLI practice in your browser. Each lab is a small network of IOS-style routers and switches and VPCS PCs, with a live topology diagram. You configure it at the console exactly as you would in EVE-NG, and every task is checked against what the network actually does: a ping only succeeds if your VLANs, trunks, routes and ACLs really let it through.

What the simulator covers

The CLI: user, privileged and every configuration mode, with ? on any word or at the end of a line, Tab completion (interface names too), abbreviations with IOS's ambiguity errors, no and do, | include/exclude/begin/section, extended ping and traceroute, debug, logging to every console, saving and reload.

CCNA: interfaces, subinterfaces and secondary addresses; VLANs, trunks, DTP; spanning tree (PVST+, Rapid PVST+, PortFast, BPDU guard); EtherChannel (LACP, PAgP); port security; router-on-a-stick; static and floating routes; single and multi-area OSPF; standard and extended ACLs; DHCP and relay; NAT and PAT; HSRP; IPv6 with SLAAC; SSH and telnet; CDP.

CCNP ENCOR: MST, root guard, loop guard, BPDU filter, UplinkFast and errdisable recovery; VTP v1–3; VRRP and GLBP; SPAN, RSPAN and ERSPAN; DHCP snooping, dynamic ARP inspection, IP source guard and private VLANs; Layer 3 switching with routing protocols; eBGP and iBGP; VRF-lite, GRE and IPsec; QoS (classify, mark, police); NTP, syslog, SNMPv3, NetFlow and Flexible NetFlow, LLDP, AAA and EEM; multicast (PIM sparse and dense mode, static RP, Auto-RP, BSR, IGMP, RPF and IGMP snooping); RESTCONF and NETCONF against IOS-XE routers from a Linux workstation (curl, netconf-console).

CCNP ENARSI: OSPF areas, LSA types, stub, totally stubby, NSSA, virtual links, summarisation and authentication; EIGRP named mode, stubs, summaries, authentication and IPv6; RIPv2 and RIPng; redistribution with route-maps, prefix-lists, tags and distribute-lists; PBR; IP SLA with tracked routes; BGP best-path, route reflectors, communities and MP-BGP; MPLS with LDP and MPLS L3VPN (VPNv4, route targets, two-label forwarding); DMVPN phases 1–3; IPv6 ACLs, uRPF and DHCPv6.

Not simulated: wireless, IS-IS, SD-WAN and SD-Access fabrics (they are configured from controllers, not the IOS CLI); use EVE-NG and the workbook labs for those. Timers are compressed: everything converges as soon as you press Enter. Commands outside this list answer with % Invalid input, just as an IOS image without the feature would.
